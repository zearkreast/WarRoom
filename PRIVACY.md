WARCOMMAND TRACKER - PRIVACY POLICY
===================================

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: WarCommand Tracker collects no personal data. There is no account,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.


WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your settings, pinned planets, dismissed hints, stratagem drill scores, and
which news and alerts you have already seen are kept in the app's own storage
on your device. They are
never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.


WHAT THE APP DOWNLOADS

The app downloads Galactic War data (planets, campaigns, Major Orders and
dispatches) from the project's read-only database, hosted by Supabase
(supabase.com). These requests carry nothing about you. As with any website,
Supabase's servers see the IP address a request comes from and may keep it in
short-lived server logs under Supabase's own privacy policy; the project does
not use these logs to identify anyone.

Links you choose to open, such as Helldivers Wiki pages, open in your web
browser and fall under that site's own policy.


NOTIFICATIONS

Alerts for new Major Orders, dispatches, captures and defenses are worked out
on your device from the war data it downloads.

On Android, the app can also receive Strohmann News while it is closed,
through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. It subscribes only to the
news topics your alert settings switch on. Google's servers handle the
device's messaging token under Google's own privacy policy; the project sends
each push to a topic and never receives or stores a device identifier.

You can turn alerts off in Settings, which also ends those subscriptions, and
your system's own notification settings always apply.


DIAGNOSTICS

Settings > Copy diagnostics puts the app version, screen size and a few
settings on your clipboard so you can include them in a bug report. Nothing is
sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.


PERMISSIONS

- Internet: to download the war data.
- Notifications: optional, for alerts. On Android the system asks you first.


CHILDREN

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.


CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app and is published at
https://github.com/zearkreast/WarRoom/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
